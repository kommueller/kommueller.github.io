# Welcome to my website!

I am a tenured Senior Lecturer in the <a href="https://www.phil.uni-mannheim.de/mkw/" target ="_blank">Institute for Media and Communication Studies</a> at University of Mannheim (since 2019). Since 2024, I am also Vice Chair of the <a href="https://www.dgpuk.de/de/fachgruppen/kommunikation-und-politik" target="_blank">DGPuK Communication and Politics Division</a>.

I am an expert in **digital political communication** and the **social and psychological dynamics of media change**. In my department, I coordinate the teaching of empirical research methods, with a particular focus on **quantitative and computational approaches**. 

My research has been published in leading journals, inculding *Journal of Communication*, *Political Communication*, and *Digital Journalism*, and has received several prestigious awards, among them the Kaid Sanders Best Article of the Year Award from the International Communication Association's Political Communication Division.

Beyond academia, I actively contribute to public debates related to my research areas. I regularly author articles and expert reports for broader audiences, deliver keynote speeches, participate in panel discussions, and provide expert commentary through various media appearances.

This page will give you an overview of my academic work. Most importantly, you can find links to open access publications and open materials complementing my research in the [publications list](./publications.html).
