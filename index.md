# Welcome to my website!

I am a tenured Senior Lecturer in the <a href="https://www.phil.uni-mannheim.de/mkw/" target ="_blank">Institute for Media and Communication Studies</a> at University of Mannheim (since 2019). Since 2024, I serve as Chair of the <a href="https://www.dgpuk.de/de/fachgruppen/kommunikation-und-politik" target="_blank">DGPuK Communication and Politics Division</a>.

My work focuses on **digital political communication**, **news use and effects**, and the **social and psychological dynamics of media and communication change**. I study the **consequences of our changing political communication landscape** for individuals, society and the functioning of democracy, putting special emphasis on questions of **social identity, social hierarchization, societal coexistence, and social coherence**. In research and teaching, I have a special focus on **quantitative and computational methodology** and contribute to methods development and validation in my areas of interest.

My research has been published in leading journals, including *Journal of Communication*, *Political Communication*, and *Digital Journalism*, and has received several awards, among them the Kaid Sanders Best Article of the Year Award from the International Communication Association's Political Communication Division.

Beyond academia, I contribute to **public debates** related to my research areas. I regularly author **articles and expert reports** for broader audiences, deliver **keynote speeches**, participate in **panel discussions**, and provide expert commentary through various **media appearances**. Should you be interested in collaborating in this context, please **do get in touch** via the contact options on my <a href="https://www.phil.uni-mannheim.de/mkw/abteilungen/akademische-ratsstellen/dr-philipp-mueller" target="_blank">department website</a>.

**This website** will give you an overview of my academic work. Importantly, you can find links to **open access** publications and **open materials** complementing my research in the [publications list](./publications.html).

Oh, and, by the way: I am **no longer active on social media**. Over the past decade and a half, I maintained both personal and professional accounts on various platforms. However, I have since come to the conclusion that the downsides of a public presence on these platforms outweigh the benefits. Consequently, I have gradually scaled back my social media usage over the last few years and recently stopped posting actively on Mastodon—the last platform where I was still active. While this development makes me genuinely sad, I currently do not see how the discourse on social media could take a turn for the better.
